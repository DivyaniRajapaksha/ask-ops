# Fees and Pricing (2024)

> ARCHIVED: this page is kept for historical reference. Current pricing is on the Fees and Pricing page.

## Payment processing

| Payment type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Domestic card | 2.9% + $0.30 per successful payment |
| International card | additional 1.5% |
| Currency conversion | additional 2% |
| ACH bank transfer | 0.8%, capped at $5.00 |

## Payouts

- **Standard payouts** (2 business days): free.
- **Instant payouts**: 1.5% of the payout amount, minimum $0.50.

## Disputes

- **Dispute fee**: $20 per dispute, not returned even if the merchant wins.
