# KYC Verification Guide

KYC ("Know Your Customer") is the identity check every merchant must pass before receiving payouts. It is required by financial regulations.

## What we ask for

**Individuals and sole traders**
- Government-issued photo ID (passport, driving licence or national ID card).
- Proof of address dated within the last 3 months (utility bill or bank statement).

**Companies**
- Company registration number and registration document.
- Photo ID for every **beneficial owner** holding 25% or more of the company.
- Proof of business address.

## How long does it take?

Most verifications finish within **2 business days**. Complex company structures can take up to 7 business days.

## What happens before KYC is complete?

- The merchant **can accept payments**, up to a total of **$2,000**.
- **Payouts are held** until verification succeeds.
- After $2,000 in volume, new payments are blocked until KYC is complete.

## Common reasons for rejection

- Blurry or cropped document photo.
- Name on ID does not match the name on the account.
- Proof of address older than 3 months.

The merchant can resubmit documents from Dashboard → Settings → Verification. There is no limit on resubmissions.
