# Fees and Pricing

These are NimbusPay's standard prices, effective **January 15, 2026**. Custom pricing for large merchants is handled by the Sales team.

## Payment processing

| Payment type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Domestic card | 2.6% + $0.25 per successful payment |
| International card | additional 1.5% |
| Currency conversion | additional 1% |
| ACH bank transfer | 0.8%, capped at $5.00 |
| SEPA bank transfer | €0.35 per payment |

Failed payments are not charged.

## Payouts

- **Standard payouts** (2 business days): free.
- **Instant payouts** (within 30 minutes): 1% of the payout amount, minimum $0.50.

## Disputes

- **Dispute fee**: $15 per dispute.
- The fee is returned if the merchant wins the dispute.

## Refunds

There is no fee to issue a refund, but the original processing fee is not returned.

## Previous pricing

Merchants who signed up before January 15, 2026 kept the old pricing until **July 1, 2026**. After that date, everyone is on the prices above.
